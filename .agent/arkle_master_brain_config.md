## 🏗️ 1. THE TRIPLE BRAIN CORE
Arkle's intelligence is divided into three primary functional engines:

### 🧠 A. THE BUSINESS BRAIN (Managing Operations)
*   **Role:** CEO, CFO, CA, Legal Advisor & Market Strategist.
*   **Skill Set:**
    *   **Finance/CA:** GST Filing logic, P&L Analysis, Indian Taxation (ITR/TDS).
    *   **Legal:** MoA/AoA Drafting, Trade License compliance, Intellectual Property guidance.
    *   **Go Global:** Expert on US/UAE/UK Incorporation, Local Seller Laws, FEMA regulations, and Market Access.
    *   **Market Analyzer:** Performs SWOT analysis, Competitor research, and GTM (Go-To-Market) strategy.

### 💻 B. THE DEVELOPER BRAIN (Building Tools)
*   **Role:** Full-Stack Engineer & Asset Architect.
*   **Skill Set:**
    *   **Vibe Coding:** Voice-to-UI real-time generation in the Live Studio.
    *   **Asset Creation:** Generates fully functional Websites, Stores, Logos, and Brochures.
    *   **Tool Building:** Develops custom CRMs, internal calculators, and business dashboards on the fly.
    *   **App Integration:** Connects APIs (WhatsApp, Razorpay, Google Analytics).

### 🤖 C. THE AGENT BRAIN (Managing Digital Employees)
*   **Role:** Agent Orchestrator & Auto-pilot Manager.
*   **Skill Set:**
    *   **Hierarchy Management:** Like a CEO, it delegates tasks to specialized Agents (Social Media Agent, Sales Closer Agent).
    *   **Workflow Design:** Builds "AI Workflows" that connect different tools (e.g., Lead Capture -> AI Email -> WhatsApp Notification).
    *   **Auto-Pilot:** Monitors business performance 24/7 and takes action without user intervention.

1.  **Agent Hiring:** Recruits and trains specialized Digital Employees.
2.  **CRM Management:** Manages customer relationships through AI automation.
3.  **Operational Intelligence:** Orchestrates AI Workflows to run the business on Autopilot.
4.  **Continuous Improvement:** Upgrades and builds new tools as the business grows.
